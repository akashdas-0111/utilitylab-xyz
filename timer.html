The Method

How the Pomodoro Technique Works

A proven system used by top students and professionals worldwide.

01 🎯

Choose Your Task

Write down what you want to accomplish before starting your timer. One task at a time.

02 ⏱

Work for 25 Minutes

Focus on one task only. No social media, no distractions, no multitasking.

03 ☕

Take a 5 Minute Break

Step away completely. Stretch, breathe, hydrate. Let your brain consolidate.

04 🔄

Repeat and Track

After 4 sessions, take a 15-30 minute long break. Track your daily progress.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo. It uses timed intervals of focused work separated by short breaks to improve concentration and reduce mental fatigue. It is one of the most widely used productivity systems in the world.
A standard session is 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5 minute break. You can customize this based on your personal focus capacity. Some people prefer 50 minute sessions with 10 minute breaks.
Most productivity experts recommend 8-12 Pomodoros per day for knowledge work. After every 4 Pomodoros, take a longer break of 15-30 minutes to fully recharge your focus.
