Guide

How to Convert CGPA to Percentage

Quick Answer: Multiply your CGPA by your university's conversion factor. The most common formula is CGPA × 9.5 (used by CBSE and many central universities). For example, 8.0 CGPA × 9.5 = 76%. Different universities use different multipliers — select yours above for an exact result.
