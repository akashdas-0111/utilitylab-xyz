Percentage Formulas Guide

Every percentage formula you need, explained simply.

% of a Number

(P ÷ 100) × N

Find 20% of 150 → (20÷100)×150 = 30

Marks Percentage

(Obtained ÷ Total) × 100

450 out of 600 → (450÷600)×100 = 75%

% Increase

((New - Old) ÷ Old) × 100

200 to 250 → ((250-200)÷200)×100 = 25%

% Decrease

((Old - New) ÷ Old) × 100

200 to 150 → ((200-150)÷200)×100 = 25%

% Difference

(|A-B| ÷ avg(A,B)) × 100

Between 120 and 150 → 22.2%

Reverse % (Original)

Final ÷ (1 + P÷100)

250 after 25% increase → original = 200

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 20% of 150 = (20 × 150) ÷ 100 = 30. Use the first calculator tab above for instant results.
Percentage increase = ((New Value - Old Value) ÷ Old Value) × 100. For example, if a price goes from 200 to 250, the increase is ((250-200) ÷ 200) × 100 = 25%.
Marks percentage = (Marks Obtained ÷ Total Marks) × 100. For example, if you scored 450 out of 600, your percentage is (450 ÷ 600) × 100 = 75%.
Percentage difference measures how much two values differ relative to their average. Formula: (|A - B| ÷ ((A+B)÷2)) × 100. It is different from percentage change which has a clear starting value.
Divide the first number by the second and multiply by 100. For example, 30 is what percentage of 150? (30 ÷ 150) × 100 = 20%. Use the fourth tab above.
