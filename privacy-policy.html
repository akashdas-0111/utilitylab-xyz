Privacy Policy

This privacy policy explains exactly what data we collect, how it is used, and how we protect your privacy when you use our website.

1. Introduction and Who We Are

UtilityLab ("we", "our", or "us") operates the website located at https://utilitylab.xyz. We provide free study timers, student calculators, and educational resource reviews for college students. We are committed to transparency and respecting your privacy.

2. What Data We Collect

We believe in data minimization and do not ask you to register or log in to use our core utilities. However, we collect certain details in the following circumstances:

Email Subscriptions: If you sign up to receive our newsletter or download the Student Toolkit PDF, we collect the email address you provide.

Analytics Data: We use Google Analytics to gather anonymous metrics on how visitors navigate our site. This includes browser type, device info, referrers, and pages viewed, but none of this identifies you personally.

AdSense Cookies: Google AdSense uses cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to our website or other sites on the internet.

3. How We Use That Data

We use the data we collect for the following purposes:

- To deliver our Student Toolkit PDF to your email address.
- To analyze site usage and improve our tools, navigation, and user experience.
- To serve relevant advertisements that fund our free hosting and services.
- To send periodic email updates (if you have opted in). You can opt out at any time.

4. Google AdSense and Cookie Usage

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on UtilityLab. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You can choose to opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings page. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

5. Google Analytics Usage

We use Google Analytics to track site traffic trends. Google Analytics uses cookies to store anonymous user interactions. The details stored do not contain personal identifier records (like names, phone numbers, or mailing addresses). You can prevent Google Analytics from tracking your visits by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. Affiliate Link Disclosure

Some links on this site may be affiliate links. This means that if you click on the link and purchase a service or sign up, we may receive an affiliate commission at no extra cost to you. We only recommend tools that we have thoroughly tested and believe provide genuine value to Indian students.

7. Your Rights

Even if you do not have an account, you have rights regarding your data:

- You can request to view what email addresses we have saved.
- You can ask us to delete your email address from our subscriber list entirely.
- You can unsubscribe from our emails at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send you.

8. How to Contact Us for Data Requests

If you wish to make a data deletion request, access inquiry, or ask any questions about this privacy policy, write to us at:
Email: hello@utilitylab.xyz

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect modifications in our tools or third-party cookies. We recommend checking this page periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
